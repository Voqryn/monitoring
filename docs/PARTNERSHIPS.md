# Voqryn partner recommendation policy

Affiliate provider selection is **deferred**. No Proton or registrar affiliate links are activated by this document.

## Natural contextual recommendations

- Domain Checker / Domain Watch: domain registration services; optionally Proton Mail custom-domain email guidance after registration.
- DNS and email diagnostics: Proton Mail guidance on MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, where genuinely relevant.
- API credentials and account security: optional Proton Pass educational links.
- VPN/network privacy guides: Proton VPN where directly relevant; do not insert VPN ads into uptime or TLS incident reports.

## Rules

1. Keep diagnostic results neutral and never change status based on affiliate arrangements.
2. Label commercial referrals clearly and provide an independent/non-affiliate alternative when useful.
3. No popups or referral prompts in incident alerts; recommendations belong in guides and relevant post-action pages.
4. Confirm whether any existing Proton Partners account authorizes promotion on Voqryn before using tracking links.
5. Do not claim a domain is registrable merely because RDAP reports no record.
6. No affiliate partner is required for the core product to work.
